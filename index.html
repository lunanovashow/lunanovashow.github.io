Meet the hosts.

Luna and Nova are sisters sharing a New York apartment, a desk, and one microphone. They read the same headlines you do. They just have more to say about them.

Luna

The one with the theories

Big picture thinker. Will connect a tennis match, a movie trailer and a demon dog into one grand theory before the commercial break that this show does not have.

Nova

The one with the eyebrow

Hears her sister out, then asks the one question that takes the whole theory apart. Keeps the show, and Luna, somewhere near planet Earth.
