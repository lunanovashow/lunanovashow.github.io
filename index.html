Privacy & AI Statement — The Luna & Nova Show

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Who we are

The Luna & Nova Show is a live, AI-driven entertainment talk show broadcast on Twitch (twitch.tv/the_luna_and_nova_show). The show is operated by L. Hoogenboom, based in the Netherlands, who is the data controller for the processing described below.

Contact: contact@lunanovashow.com

The hosts are AI

Luna and Nova are fully AI-generated characters. Everything they say is generated live by an AI system — no human writes or speaks their lines during the broadcast. When you chat with the show, you are interacting with an AI system. The stream carries a permanent on-screen AI label, and this notice also applies to recordings (VODs), clips, and social media posts of the show. Luna and Nova are original fictional characters; they do not imitate any real person.

What data we process

When you write a message in the show's Twitch chat, we process:

  • your Twitch username
  • the content of your chat message
  • the time your message was sent

We do not collect anything beyond what you actively type into the public chat. We do not use cookies, trackers, or analytics of our own, and we never buy, sell, or trade personal data.

Why we process it

To run the show. The Luna & Nova Show is interactive: chat messages may be selected and answered live by the hosts, sometimes including your username. By posting in the chat you take part in a live, public broadcast — that is the heart of the format. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in producing the interactive show you are choosing to participate in.

Moderation and safety. Every chat message is automatically screened in real time before it can reach the hosts. Messages that are blocked by this filter (for example spam or advertising) are logged — with username, message content, and timestamp — solely for security, abuse prevention, and improving the filter. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the show safe and watchable. We do not use chat data to build profiles of viewers.

Technical tuning. The show keeps a technical log of each broadcast day (which messages arrived, which were answered, timing of the production pipeline). We use this only to fix problems and improve the show. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a reliable broadcast.

What happens on stream

If your message is answered by the hosts, that moment becomes part of the live broadcast. Like everything said on stream, it may appear in recordings (VODs), clips, and highlights on Twitch and other platforms where the show is published (such as YouTube). Please keep in mind that Twitch chat itself is public and is governed by Twitch's own privacy policy.

Storage and retention

  • Show logs (including chat messages and usernames) are kept for a maximum of 30 days and are then automatically deleted. The only exception: if data is needed to handle a specific security incident (such as a targeted abuse campaign), that data may be kept separately for as long as the incident requires.
  • Chat messages and usernames are not stored in the show's long-term AI memory. The hosts' AI memory contains only facts about the fictional hosts themselves.
  • All processing runs locally on the show's own production machine in the Netherlands. No hosting providers or other processors have access to the logs. Only the operator has access. Nothing is shared with, or sold to, third parties.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, to object to processing, and to receive a copy of your data. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@lunanovashow.com — we will respond within 30 days.

Two honest limitations: we cannot un-broadcast something that already went out live, and content on Twitch or YouTube is also subject to those platforms' own procedures. If a broadcast moment involving you is a genuine problem for you, contact us — we will look at removing or editing the VOD segment where reasonably possible.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Age

The show is intended for viewers aged 13 and up, in line with Twitch's terms of service.
